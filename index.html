The Beginning

VastraHub was born from a simple yet powerful vision — to bring India's magnificent textile heritage to the world. Founded in 2014 in the heart of Hyderabad, we started as a small curation of Kanjeevaram silks and Banarasi brocades, working directly with master weavers who had spent decades perfecting their craft.

What began as a passion project has grown into a movement. Today, we partner with over 500 artisan families across 28 states, ensuring fair wages, preserving dying crafts, and creating a sustainable marketplace for authentic Indian textiles.

Empowering Artisans

At the heart of VastraHub are the artisans — the master weavers, dyers, embroiderers, and craftsmen who pour their soul into every thread. We work directly with these artisans, cutting out middlemen to ensure they receive fair compensation for their work.

Beyond just commerce, we invest in artisan communities through skill development programs, healthcare initiatives, and educational scholarships for the next generation. By preserving these crafts, we are not only keeping traditions alive but also empowering families to thrive in a modern economy.
